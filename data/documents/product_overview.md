# TechNova Solutions: Product Overview

TechNova Solutions builds software for mid-sized businesses. We currently
have two products.

## 1. NovaDesk

NovaDesk is a customer support platform. It collects customer tickets from
email and chat in one place and lets support teams assign, track and resolve
them. Key features: ticket routing, canned replies, and a customer
satisfaction survey after each resolved ticket.

## 2. NovaInsight

NovaInsight is a business analytics platform. It connects to a company's
databases and spreadsheets and turns the data into dashboards. Key
features: scheduled reports, shareable dashboards, and email alerts when
a metric crosses a threshold.
