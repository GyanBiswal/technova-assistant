# TechNova Solutions: New Employee Onboarding Guide

## 1. Before Day One

HR sends the offer documents and joining checklist one week before the
joining date. New employees must bring government ID proof and bank
account details on the first day.

## 2. First Day

- 10:00 AM: Welcome session with HR at the Bengaluru office.
- 11:30 AM: Laptop and access card are issued by the IT team.
- 2:00 PM: Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy.

## 3. Onboarding Buddy

Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from their own team.
The buddy answers day-to-day questions and helps for the first 3 months.

## 4. IT Setup

New employees receive a company email account, VPN access, and access to
the HR portal and finance portal on day one. Laptop issues should be
reported to it-support@technova.example.

## 5. Probation Period

The probation period is 3 months from the joining date. A review meeting
with the manager is held at the end of month 3, after which employment
is confirmed in writing.

## 6. Mandatory Training

Within the first 2 weeks, every new employee must complete three training
modules: Information Security Awareness, Code of Conduct, and Workplace
Safety. Completion is tracked in the HR portal.

## 7. First 90 Days

- Days 1-30: Learn the team, tools and products.
- Days 31-60: Take ownership of small tasks.
- Days 61-90: Work independently and prepare for the probation review.
