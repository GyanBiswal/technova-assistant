# TechNova Solutions: Leave Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Applies to all full-time employees.

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid leave per calendar year:
- Casual Leave: 8 days
- Sick Leave: 8 days
- Earned Leave: 8 days

Leave balances reset on 1 January. Casual and sick leave do not carry over.
Up to 5 days of unused earned leave may be carried over to the next year.

## 2. Casual Leave

Casual leave is for personal errands and short-notice needs. A maximum of
3 consecutive casual leave days may be taken at one time. Employees should
inform their manager at least 1 working day in advance where possible.

## 3. Sick Leave

Sick leave can be taken when an employee is unwell. A medical certificate
is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days. Employees must notify
their manager before 10:00 AM on the day of absence.

## 4. Earned Leave

Earned leave is for planned vacations. It must be applied for at least
7 days in advance through the HR portal and requires manager approval.
Earned leave cannot be encashed during employment.

## 5. Maternity and Paternity Leave

- Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave.
- Paternity leave: 10 working days of paid leave, to be taken within
  3 months of the child's birth.

## 6. Public Holidays

TechNova observes 12 public holidays per year. The list is published by
HR every December for the following year.

## 7. Contact

Questions about leave can be sent to hr@technova.example.
