# TechNova Solutions: Work From Home Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Applies to all full-time employees.

## 1. Hybrid Work Model

TechNova follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office 3 days
per week and may work from home 2 days per week. Office days are Tuesday,
Wednesday and Thursday unless the team agrees otherwise.

## 2. Core Hours

Regardless of location, employees must be available online between
11:00 AM and 4:00 PM IST on working days.

## 3. Eligibility

Employees become eligible for work from home after completing their
3-month probation period. Employees on probation work from the office
5 days per week.

## 4. Home Internet Allowance

Eligible employees receive a home internet allowance of Rs 1,000 per
month. Claims are submitted under the Expense Policy.

## 5. Temporary Full-Remote Requests

An employee may request up to 10 fully remote days per year for special
circumstances, such as relocation or family needs. These require approval
from both the manager and HR.

## 6. Security Requirements

Employees working from home must use the company VPN and must not use
public Wi-Fi for work. Company laptops must not be shared with others.
