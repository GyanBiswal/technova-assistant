# TechNova Solutions: Expense Reimbursement Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026.

## 1. Submission Deadline

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date through
the finance portal. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed.

## 2. Receipts

A receipt or invoice is required for every expense above Rs 500. Expenses
of Rs 500 or less may be claimed with a short description instead.

## 3. Approval Limits

- Claims up to Rs 10,000: approved by the employee's manager.
- Claims above Rs 10,000: require approval from the department head.

## 4. Travel and Meals

During approved business travel, employees may claim a meal allowance of
up to Rs 1,500 per day. Economy class applies to all flights. Hotel stays
are capped at Rs 6,000 per night.

## 5. Home Internet Allowance Claims

Employees eligible for work from home may claim Rs 1,000 per month for
home internet. Submit the monthly bill as proof, under the category
"WFH Allowance".

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Items

Personal entertainment, fines, penalties, and alcohol are not reimbursable.

## 7. Contact

Questions can be sent to finance@technova.example.
