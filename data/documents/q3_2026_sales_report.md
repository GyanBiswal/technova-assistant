# TechNova Solutions: Q3 2026 Sales Report

Reporting period: 1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026.
All amounts are in Indian rupees (Rs). 1 lakh = Rs 100,000.

## 1. Summary

Total revenue for Q3 2026 was Rs 230 lakh, against a target of Rs 220 lakh.
This is 15% higher than Q2 2026, when revenue was Rs 200 lakh.
TechNova added 46 new customers during the quarter.

## 2. Revenue by Region and Product (Rs lakh)

| Region | NovaDesk | NovaInsight | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| North | 30 | 25 | 55 |
| South | 45 | 40 | 85 |
| East | 15 | 10 | 25 |
| West | 35 | 30 | 65 |
| Total | 125 | 105 | 230 |

## 3. Highlights

- South was the strongest region, contributing Rs 85 lakh.
- NovaDesk earned more than NovaInsight in every region.
- East was the weakest region at Rs 25 lakh.

## 4. Risks and Next Quarter

East region revenue is below plan. The sales team will hire one additional
account executive in East during Q4. The Q4 2026 revenue target is
Rs 250 lakh.
